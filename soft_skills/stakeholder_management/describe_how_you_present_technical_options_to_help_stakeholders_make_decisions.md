When presenting technical options to stakeholders, I translate technical complexity into business language and structure the information for decision-making. The goal is not to make stakeholders into technical experts, but to give them the information they need to make an informed business decision.

I present options in a structured format. For each option, I cover: what it is (in plain language), the pros, the cons, the cost (time, money, resources), and the risks. I use a comparison table when there are multiple options, which makes it easy to see the trade-offs side by side. For example:

Option A: Use a third-party payment provider
- Pros: Fast to implement (2 weeks), maintained by vendor, PCI compliance handled
- Cons: Transaction fees, less customization, vendor lock-in risk
- Cost: 2 weeks of development, 2.9% per transaction
- Risk: Vendor outage affects our checkout

Option B: Build custom payment integration
- Pros: Full control, no transaction fees, custom UX
- Cons: Longer to build (8 weeks), we handle PCI compliance, ongoing maintenance
- Cost: 8 weeks of development, ongoing maintenance
- Risk: Security vulnerabilities if not implemented correctly

I always include a recommendation with my reasoning. Stakeholders appreciate a clear recommendation backed by analysis—they don't want to make the technical decision themselves, they want to trust that I've thought it through. I say: "I recommend Option A because it meets our current needs, gets us to market faster, and the transaction fees are minimal compared to the development cost. We can revisit Option B if our volume justifies the investment."

I also consider what each stakeholder cares about. The CFO cares about cost. The CEO cares about time to market. The Head of Support cares about reliability. I frame each option in terms of what matters to the audience, not just what matters to me as a developer. A technically superior option that takes twice as long isn't superior from a business perspective if the deadline matters more than the elegance of the solution.
