Handling bias in AI models requires a systematic approach across the entire ML pipeline—data collection, model development, evaluation, and deployment. Bias can enter at any stage, and mitigation requires vigilance at each step.

Data-level mitigation starts with understanding your training data. Analyze demographic representation—are certain groups underrepresented? Check for labeling bias—do annotators consistently label similar examples differently based on demographic signals? Collect diverse, representative data. Use techniques like oversampling underrepresented groups, undersampling overrepresented groups, or generating synthetic data for underrepresented categories. Document data sources and known limitations.

Model-level mitigation includes fairness-aware algorithms that incorporate fairness constraints during training. Adversarial debiasing trains the model to make accurate predictions while preventing it from predicting protected attributes. Pre-processing techniques transform data to remove bias before training. Post-processing techniques adjust model outputs to equalize metrics across groups. Regularization terms can penalize disparate impact.

Evaluation is critical—test model performance across demographic groups. Metrics include equalized odds (equal true positive and false positive rates across groups), demographic parity (equal prediction rates), and calibration (equal accuracy across groups). No single fairness metric is universally appropriate—choose metrics based on your application's context and potential harms. Test with disaggregated data—overall accuracy can mask poor performance for specific subgroups.

Ongoing monitoring tracks bias in production. Monitor prediction distributions across groups, track user complaints by demographic, and regularly audit model outputs. Implement feedback loops—when users report biased outcomes, investigate and retrain. Establish diverse review teams who can identify biases that homogeneous teams might miss. Fairness is not a one-time fix but an ongoing process that requires continuous attention and improvement.
